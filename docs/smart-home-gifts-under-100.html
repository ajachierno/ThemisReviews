Gift-buying questions

What is the best smart home gift under $100?

For most people, a video doorbell or a smart thermostat, because they get used every day. If they're just starting out, a hub-free starter kit, like a Lutron Caseta dimmer kit or a set of Matter plugs, is an easy first step.

Should I match the brand they already own?

If you know their system, yes: pick a device with their system's icon. Matter devices work with Apple Home, Google Home, Alexa, SmartThings, and Home Assistant, so they're the safe choice when you're not sure.

Where are the gifts under $50?

They're on our Smart Home Gifts Under $50 page. This page only lists picks between $50 and $100, so the two pages don't repeat each other.
