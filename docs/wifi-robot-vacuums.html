Buyer's guide

Do I need a self-emptying dock?

If you have pets or long hair in the house, yes. A dock that empties the bin means you deal with dirt every month or two instead of every run.

Is a mopping robot worth it?

For hard floors, a robot that mops and washes its own mop pads saves real work. Pick one that lifts the mop on carpet, or keep it off rugs.

Does it work with my smart home?

Most robot vacuums use their maker's Wi-Fi app with Alexa and Google voice commands. Newer models add Matter, which lets Apple Home, SmartThings, and Home Assistant start and stop cleaning.
