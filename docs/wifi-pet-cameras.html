Buyer's guide

Do I need a subscription?

Many pet cameras lock barking alerts, cloud video, and AI features behind a monthly plan. Check what the free tier includes; some Furbo listings require a plan just to activate.

Treat tossing or just a camera?

Treat cameras let you reward your pet remotely. A regular pan-and-tilt indoor camera is much cheaper if you only want to watch and talk.

Will it work on my Wi-Fi?

Most need 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi; some also support 5 GHz. Put the camera within good range of your router.
