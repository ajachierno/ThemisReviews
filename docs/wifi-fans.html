Buyer's guide

Tower fan or pedestal fan?

Tower fans are slim and quiet and suit bedrooms and offices. Pedestal fans move more air over a longer distance and suit larger rooms.

What does a smart fan add?

App and voice control, schedules, and sometimes a temperature sensor that speeds up or slows down automatically.

DC motor or AC?

DC motors are quieter, use less power, and offer more speed steps.
