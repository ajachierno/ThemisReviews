Buyer's guide

What does Matter add to a robot vacuum?

You can start, stop, and send it home from Apple Home, Google Home, Alexa, SmartThings, or Home Assistant, and use it in automations across systems. Mapping and room selection still live in the maker's app.

Do Matter vacuums need a hub?

Wi-Fi Matter vacuums need a Matter controller such as an Apple TV, HomePod, Nest Hub, or Echo.

Self-emptying base: worth it?

Yes, if you don't want to empty the bin every run. A base holds weeks of dust in a bag.
