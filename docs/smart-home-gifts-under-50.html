Gift-buying questions

What is a good smart home gift under $50?

For someone new to smart homes, a smart plug or a pack of smart bulbs is the easiest win: they work over Wi-Fi with Alexa or Google and need no hub. For someone who already has a system, a sensor, button, or tracker that matches their hub is a better fit.

How do I know it will work with their system?

Look at the Works with icons on each pick. If you don't know what they use, Wi-Fi and Matter devices are the safest choices, because they work with the most platforms. Zigbee and Z-Wave devices need a compatible hub.

What if they already have it?

Every gift links to Amazon, where you can add a gift receipt at checkout. Smart plugs, bulbs, and sensors are hard to own too many of, so they make safe picks.
