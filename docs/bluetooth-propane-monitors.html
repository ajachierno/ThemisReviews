Buyer's guide

How do propane tank monitors work?

Most stick to the bottom of the tank and measure the liquid level with ultrasound; others replace the gauge on large tanks. The app shows the level and alerts you before you run out.

Bluetooth or Wi-Fi?

Bluetooth sensors only update when your phone is nearby. Wi-Fi models or a Wi-Fi bridge send alerts from anywhere.

Will it fit my tank?

Check the tank sizes listed. Grill tanks (20 lb) and large home tanks usually need different models.
