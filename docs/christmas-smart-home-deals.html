When is Christmas 2026?

Friday, December 25, 2026. Shipping cutoffs land in the days before, so order early in the week of December 21 if you need it under the tree.

What's a good last-minute smart home gift?

Something that works without a hub: a Wi-Fi smart plug, a smart bulb, a video doorbell or an indoor camera. Check our gift pages for the best pick under $50 and under $100 in each device type.

Are Christmas prices lower than Black Friday?

Usually not. Black Friday and Cyber Monday tend to have the lowest prices of the season. Some devices drop again in mid-December, so compare today's price with the regular price in our cheat sheet.

What if the gift needs a hub?

Z-Wave and Zigbee devices need a hub or controller to work. If you're not sure what the recipient uses, pick a Wi-Fi or Matter device; Matter works with Apple Home, Google Home, Alexa and SmartThings.
