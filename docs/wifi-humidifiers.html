Buyer's guide

What humidity should my home be?

Aim for 40 to 50 percent. Below 30 percent, skin, sinuses, and wood floors dry out; above 60 percent, mold and dust mites thrive. A smart humidifier holds a target level automatically.

Warm mist or cool mist?

Cool mist uses less power and is safer around kids. Warm mist feels nicer in winter and boils the water, but uses more electricity. Some models do both.

What does the smart part add?

Remote control, schedules, a humidity target, refill and filter alerts, and voice control through Alexa or Google.
