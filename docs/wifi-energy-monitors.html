Buyer's guide

Do I need an electrician?

Whole-home monitors clip current sensors around wires inside your electrical panel. Many people install them, but it means working inside a live panel. Hire an electrician if you're not comfortable.

Whole-home or circuit-level?

Two main sensors show your total use. Adding circuit sensors shows which appliances use what, like the dryer, AC, or EV charger.

Does it work with solar?

Many monitors can track solar production and net metering with extra sensors. Check the listing if you have panels.
