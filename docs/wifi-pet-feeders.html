Buyer's guide

Dry food only?

Almost all automatic feeders handle dry kibble only. Check the kibble size limits in the listing.

What if the power or Wi-Fi goes out?

Look for battery backup. Most feeders keep their schedule locally and keep feeding without Wi-Fi; you just lose app control and alerts.

Do I need the camera version?

A camera lets you watch your pet eat and confirms the food actually dropped. If you just want reliable meals, the non-camera version is cheaper.
