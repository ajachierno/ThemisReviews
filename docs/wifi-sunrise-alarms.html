Buyer's guide

Do sunrise alarms actually help?

Many people wake up more easily to light that brightens over 20 to 30 minutes before the alarm, especially in dark winter months. It's a gentler start, not a cure for poor sleep.

What does the app add?

Schedules for weekdays and weekends, custom sounds and colors, and wind-down routines without touching the clock. Some lock premium sounds and content behind a subscription.

Are they good for kids?

Yes. Many double as a sound machine and a stay-in-bed light that turns green when it's OK to get up.
