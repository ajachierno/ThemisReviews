Buyer's guide

How do mailbox sensors work?

A small sensor inside the mailbox detects the door opening or motion and sends an alert to your phone. Metal mailboxes block radio signals, so most use long-range radio with an indoor hub or receiver.

Will it reach my house?

Check the stated range. Long-range models reach a quarter mile or more; Wi-Fi models need Wi-Fi coverage at the curb.

Do they need a hub?

Most do. The hub plugs in indoors and passes alerts to the app.
