Buyer's guide

What can a smart button do?

Anything your hub can run: turn on a scene, toggle a lamp, start a routine, or arm an alarm. Many buttons recognize single, double, and long presses, so one button can run three actions.

Do buttons need a hub?

Yes, for Zigbee and Z-Wave. The button sends a press to the hub, and the hub runs the automation. Some Zigbee buttons can bind directly to a bulb or plug, but most setups go through the hub.

How long do the batteries last?

Most battery sensors on these lists are rated for one to three years, depending on how often they report. Low-power radios like Zigbee, Z-Wave, and Thread last much longer than Wi-Fi.
