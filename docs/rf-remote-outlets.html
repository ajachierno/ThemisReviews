Buyer's guide

What's an RF remote outlet?

A plug-in outlet switched by a small handheld radio remote, with no Wi-Fi, app, or hub. It's the simplest way to switch lamps, holiday lights, and hard-to-reach outlets.

How far does the remote reach?

Usually 80-100 feet, and through walls, because 433 MHz radio passes through them better than Wi-Fi.

Can I make them work with Alexa or Home Assistant?

Not directly. An RF bridge, such as the ones on our RF bridges page, can learn the remote's codes and add them to a smart home system.
