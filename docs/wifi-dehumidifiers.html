Buyer's guide

What size dehumidifier do I need?

Capacity is measured in pints of water removed per day. Around 20-35 pints suits a bedroom or small basement; 50 pints suits a large damp basement. Bigger units also cover more square feet.

Can it drain on its own?

Most have a hose port for continuous drainage into a floor drain, and some have a built-in pump to push water up into a sink. Otherwise you empty the bucket.

What does the smart part add?

A full-bucket alert on your phone, a humidity target, schedules, and remote control, which matters most for a basement you don't visit every day.
