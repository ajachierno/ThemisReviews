Gift-buying questions

Are the most expensive smart home devices worth it?

Sometimes. Premium models usually add better build quality, more sensors, longer range, or support for more systems. But we only picked products that also score near the top of their category on ratings and features, so nothing here is expensive for the sake of it.

What's the best high-end smart home gift for someone who has everything?

Pick something that upgrades a system they already use: a premium smart lock, a floodlight camera, a mesh router, or a top-end thermostat. The Works with icons tell you whether it fits their Alexa, Google, Apple Home, or Home Assistant setup.

Will a premium device work with their existing system?

Check the Works with icons. Matter devices work with the widest range of systems. Zigbee and Z-Wave devices need a compatible hub, and some premium brands work best inside their own app.
