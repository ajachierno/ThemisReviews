Buyer's guide

Will it work with my garage door opener?

Most controllers wire to the opener's wall-button terminals, which works with almost any opener with a standard push button. Newer openers with encrypted wall buttons may need an adapter; check the listing.

Is it safe to close the door remotely?

Good controllers flash and beep before closing, as UL 325 requires, and use a tilt sensor to confirm the door's position. Never close a door you can't see without that warning.

Does it need a hub?

Z-Wave controllers need a Z-Wave hub. Matter controllers work with Apple Home, Google Home, Alexa, SmartThings, and Home Assistant.
