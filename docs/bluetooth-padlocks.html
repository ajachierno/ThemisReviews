Buyer's guide

How does a Bluetooth padlock open?

With your phone over Bluetooth, a fingerprint on some models, or a backup code or key. You can share access with others through the app.

Is it as strong as a regular padlock?

The shackle and body matter more than the electronics. Look for a hardened steel shackle and a weather rating for outdoor use.

What if the battery dies?

Most warn you well before and charge over USB. Many also have a key or jump-start contacts as a backup.
