How this is measured

A check is the price Amazon showed for a listing on the day one of our pages was refreshed. Each page is re-priced about weekly, so most products have one check per week. The 14-day change compares the latest check with the last check taken at least 14 days earlier; products without a check that old are left out rather than guessed. Moves under 3% or under $2.00 are treated as noise. A monthly report compares each product's last check before the month with its last check inside it. We only track products currently ranked on the site; when one is removed, its history stops.
