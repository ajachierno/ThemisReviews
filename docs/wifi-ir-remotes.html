Buyer's guide

What can an IR blaster control?

Anything that uses an infrared remote: TVs, air conditioners, fans, fireplaces, soundbars, and older electronics. It needs a clear line of sight to the device.

Will it know if my TV is on?

Usually not. IR is one-way, so the blaster sends a command but can't confirm the device received it. Some models add a sensor cable or power monitoring to guess the state.

IR or RF?

IR covers most TVs and AC units. Some ceiling fans, shades, and outlets use radio (RF) remotes, which need a model with RF support.
