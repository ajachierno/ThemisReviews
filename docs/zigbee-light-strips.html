Buyer's guide

RGB, RGBIC, or RGBWW: what's the difference?

RGB strips show one color at a time along the whole strip. RGBIC strips can show several colors at once, for rainbow and chase effects. RGBWW strips add real white LEDs, which look much better for everyday lighting than white mixed from red, green, and blue.

Can I cut a light strip?

Most strips can be cut at the marked lines, but the cut-off piece usually can't be reconnected without connectors. Measure first and buy close to the length you need.

Do light strips need a hub?

Wi-Fi strips connect to your router. Hue and other Zigbee strips need a Zigbee hub or bridge. Matter strips work with Apple Home, Google Home, Alexa, SmartThings, and Home Assistant.
