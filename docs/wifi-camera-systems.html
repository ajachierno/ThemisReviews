Buyer's guide

Camera system or individual cameras?

A system records every camera to one recorder (NVR) in your home, usually continuously and with no monthly fee. Individual cameras are easier to install but usually record motion clips to the cloud or an SD card.

PoE or Wi-Fi cameras?

PoE cameras get power and data over one Ethernet cable, which is more reliable and needs no outlet near the camera. Wi-Fi systems are easier to install but depend on signal strength.

Do they work with Home Assistant?

Many NVR systems support RTSP streams, and Reolink has an official Home Assistant integration. Check each pick's notes.
