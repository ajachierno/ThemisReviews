Buyer's guide

Belt drive or chain drive?

Belt drives are much quieter, which matters if there's a bedroom over the garage. Chain drives cost less and handle heavy doors well.

Do I need a smart opener or just a smart controller?

If your opener works, a smart controller like myQ or meross adds app control for far less. Replace the whole opener if it's old, noisy, or lacks safety sensors.

Does myQ work with Home Assistant or Google?

Chamberlain's myQ works with its own app, Amazon Key, and some car brands, but no longer supports Home Assistant or Google Home directly. Genie's Aladdin Connect works with Alexa and Google.
