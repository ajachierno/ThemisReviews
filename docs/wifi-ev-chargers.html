Buyer's guide

Plug-in or hardwired?

Plug-in chargers use a NEMA 14-50 outlet and can be moved. Hardwired chargers can deliver more current (48 A or more) and are required for some installs. Both need a 240 V circuit.

How many amps do I need?

A 40 A charger adds about 30 miles of range per hour on most EVs, which covers overnight charging for nearly everyone. Your car's onboard charger may cap the speed anyway.

What does smart add?

Scheduling for cheap off-peak electricity, charging history, and sometimes utility rebates. Check your utility for EV charger rebates before buying.
