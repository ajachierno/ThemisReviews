Buyer's guide

What can I plug into a smart plug?

Lamps, fans, coffee makers, holiday lights, and anything else that turns on with a physical switch left in the on position. Check the plug's amp rating (most are 15 A) before plugging in anything that draws a lot of power.

Is energy monitoring worth paying for?

If you want to know what a fridge, dehumidifier, or entertainment center costs to run, yes. Energy-monitoring plugs also let you trigger automations, like an alert when the washer finishes.

Will a smart plug block the second outlet?

Bulky plugs often cover the other socket. Mini designs leave it free, so check the shape before you buy several.

Are smart plugs safe for heaters and air conditioners?

Only if the plug's rating covers the appliance's draw and the maker allows it. Many plugs are rated 15 A, but heaters run near that limit for hours. Check the appliance's manual; some say not to use any timer or smart plug.
