Buyer's guide

Portable or window AC?

Window units cool better for the money. Portable units suit windows that can't take a window unit, like casement or sliding windows, or rentals that don't allow them.

Single hose or dual hose?

Dual-hose units pull outside air to cool the condenser, so they don't create negative pressure that draws warm air in through gaps. They cool large rooms faster.

What does Wi-Fi add?

Cool the room before you get home, schedules, and Alexa or Google voice control.
