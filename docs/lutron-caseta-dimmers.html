Buyer's guide

Do Caseta switches need a neutral wire?

Most Caseta dimmers don't, which is the main reason people pick them for older houses. The on/off switch and the fan control need a neutral, so check the model before you buy.

Do I need the Smart Bridge?

For app control, schedules, voice assistants, Apple Home, or Home Assistant, yes. A Caseta switch with a Pico remote works without one. Several kits on this list include the bridge.

How do Caseta switches handle 3-way circuits?

Lutron uses a Pico remote in place of the second switch. You cap the traveler wire at the other box and mount a Pico in a wallplate bracket there. No extra wiring is needed.

Can I install it myself?

If you're comfortable replacing an ordinary switch, the job is similar: power off at the breaker, confirm it's dead with a tester, and match the wires. Check local code, and hire an electrician if the box is crowded, the wiring is aluminum, or you're not sure which wire is which.
