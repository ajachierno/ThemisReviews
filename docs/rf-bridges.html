Buyer's guide

What does an RF bridge do?

It learns the signals from 433 MHz remotes, doorbells, and sensors so you can trigger them from an app or a smart home hub. Most handle only fixed-code remotes, not the rolling-code remotes used by garage doors and car fobs.

What is an SDR receiver for?

A software-defined radio USB stick can listen to many 433 MHz and 915 MHz weather sensors at once. With rtl_433 and Home Assistant, it turns cheap weather and temperature sensors into smart home data. It only receives; it can't send commands.

Is any of this plug and play?

The Wi-Fi IR/RF hubs are app-based and fairly easy. SDR sticks and custom firmware are hobbyist projects that need some comfort with Linux or Home Assistant add-ons.
