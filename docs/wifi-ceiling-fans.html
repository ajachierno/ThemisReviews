Buyer's guide

Smart ceiling fan or a smart fan control?

A smart fan has Wi-Fi in the motor and light, so you get app control, schedules, and voice control without changing your wall switch. A smart wall control makes an existing fan smart without replacing it.

Do smart ceiling fans need a hub?

Most connect over Wi-Fi to the maker's app and work with Alexa and Google. Some use Bluetooth for setup.

What size fan do I need?

Roughly 42-48 inches for a bedroom up to 150 square feet, 52 inches for a typical living room, and 60 inches or more for large open rooms.
