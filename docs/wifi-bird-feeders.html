Buyer's guide

How do smart bird feeders identify birds?

A camera takes photos or video when a bird lands, and AI in the app names the species. Some brands charge a subscription for identification or cloud video; check each pick's notes.

Will squirrels raid it?

Probably. Mount it on a pole with a baffle, away from trees and fences squirrels can jump from.

How is it powered?

Most run on a rechargeable battery, and many include or offer a solar panel so you rarely need to bring it inside.
