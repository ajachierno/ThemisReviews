Buyer's guide

What does a Wi-Fi weather station do that an RF one doesn't?

It uploads readings to the internet, so you can see your backyard conditions from anywhere, get alerts, and share data with services like Weather Underground. Many also work with Home Assistant.

Where should the outdoor sensor go?

In open air about five feet off the ground, away from walls, roofs, and pavement, which skew temperature. Wind and rain sensors need to be higher and clear of obstructions.

Does it need a hub?

Most use an indoor display or small gateway that receives the outdoor sensor by radio and connects to Wi-Fi. It's included in the kit.
