Buyer's guide

Can a 433 MHz sensor work with a smart home?

Not on its own. These sensors talk to their own display. With a cheap USB SDR receiver and the rtl_433 software, Home Assistant can read many AcuRite, La Crosse, and Ambient Weather sensors, but that's a do-it-yourself project, not a supported feature.

Will a replacement sensor work with my display?

Only if the listing names your display's model. 433 MHz sensors use brand-specific formats, so an AcuRite sensor won't pair with a La Crosse display.

Where should an outdoor sensor go?

In the shade, out of direct sun and rain, and a few feet off the ground. A sensor in the sun can read 10°F or more too high.
