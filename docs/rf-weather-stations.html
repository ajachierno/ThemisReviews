Buyer's guide

Can a 433 MHz weather station join my smart home?

Not by itself. These stations send to their own console. Some models add Wi-Fi to upload to weather services, and Home Assistant can read many of their sensors with a USB SDR and rtl_433, which is a do-it-yourself project.

Where should the outdoor sensor go?

In open air, away from roofs, walls, and pavement that radiate heat, ideally 5 ft. or more above the ground. Wind sensors need to be above nearby obstacles to read accurately.

What does 5-in-1 or 7-in-1 mean?

The number of measurements the outdoor sensor takes: usually temperature, humidity, wind speed, wind direction, and rainfall, with light and UV added in 7-in-1 models.
