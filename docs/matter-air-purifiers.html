Buyer's guide

What size purifier do I need?

Match the room size to the purifier's rated coverage, ideally with some headroom. CADR (clean air delivery rate) is the better number to compare when listings give it.

What does smart add?

App and voice control, schedules, filter reminders, and an air quality sensor that turns the fan up when the air gets worse. Matter models work with every major platform.

How much do filters cost?

Replacement filters usually run $15 to $60 and last 6 to 12 months. Check the filter price before you buy the purifier.
