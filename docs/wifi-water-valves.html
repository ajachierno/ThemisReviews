Buyer's guide

Do I need a plumber?

Not for valve robots and actuators, which clamp onto your existing quarter-turn main valve. Inline valves and whole-home flow monitors like Moen Flo are installed into the pipe and usually need a plumber.

How does it know to shut off?

Pair it with leak sensors, or pick a flow monitor that spots unusual water use. When a sensor gets wet, the valve closes the main line in seconds.

Will it work with my valve?

Robots fit most quarter-turn ball valves, but not gate valves with round handles. Check your main valve and pipe size before ordering.
