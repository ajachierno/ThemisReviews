Buyer's guide

How accurate are body composition scales?

Weight is accurate to a few tenths of a pound. Body fat and muscle numbers from foot sensors are estimates; use them to see trends, not exact values.

Wi-Fi or Bluetooth?

Wi-Fi scales sync on their own every time you step on. Bluetooth scales need your phone nearby with the app open.

Does it work with Apple Health and Google Fit?

Most do, along with apps like Fitbit or MyFitnessPal. Check the listing for the ones you use.
