Buyer's guide

Does a smart controller save water?

Usually. Weather-based skipping and seasonal adjustment cut watering when it rains or cools down, and many water utilities offer rebates for EPA WaterSense controllers.

How many zones do I need?

Count the zones on your current controller and buy at least that many. Extra zones give room to add drip lines or new beds later.

Indoor or outdoor model?

Indoor models mount in a garage. If your current controller is outside, buy an outdoor-rated model or one with a weatherproof enclosure.
