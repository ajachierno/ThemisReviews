Buyer's guide

Smart lamp or a smart bulb in a regular lamp?

A smart lamp builds the color effects into the fixture, often with segmented RGBIC light a single bulb can't do. A smart bulb is cheaper if you like the lamp you have.

Do smart lamps need a hub?

Most Wi-Fi lamps connect straight to your router. Philips Hue lamps need the Hue Bridge for full features.

Can they light a whole room?

Floor lamps with 1,000+ lumens can. Many color lamps are accent lights, so check the brightness if you need reading light.
